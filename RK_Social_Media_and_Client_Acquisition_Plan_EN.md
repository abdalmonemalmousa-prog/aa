## 1. Executive Summary

The purpose of this plan is to turn the firm's digital presence from "occasional posting" into an **integrated legal marketing system** that serves three goals in parallel:

1. **Client acquisition (Lead Generation):** a continuous, measurable flow of qualified leads to the Sales and Business Development team through the CRM.
2. **Presence on Google (SEO + reputation):** the firm appears on the first page of search results for the targeted legal keywords in the UAE, and its image on Google (Business Profile, reviews, Knowledge Panel, news) is professional and under control.
3. **The founder's personal brand:** building a media "halo" around Dr. Rashid Al Kaitoob that makes him the first reference for commentary on new laws, decrees and decisions, to the point where he becomes the trend whenever major legislation is issued.

**Channel allocation logic (as defined by management):**

| Channel | Direction | Audience | Primary goal |
|---|---|---|---|
| X (formerly Twitter) | Legislative and legal commentary in Dr. Rashid Al Kaitoob's voice: laws, decrees, decisions, rulings | Decision-makers, journalists, elites, those following legislative affairs | Authority + owning the trend |
| Facebook | Mass-market, wider, simpler language, direct search for clients | Individuals, residents and small-business owners | Direct leads |
| Instagram | Brand awareness + leads | Individuals, business owners, youth and professionals | Awareness + leads |
| LinkedIn | Business-to-business (B2B) and business-to-government (B2G), company structure, expertise, international partnerships | Corporates, investors, government entities, international law firms | Institutional partnerships and contracts |
| Google | Search engine optimization + image and reputation | Everyone searching for a legal service in the UAE | First-page presence + trust |
| Website | The hub into which all channels feed | Everyone | Conversion and the knowledge archive |
| YouTube / Podcast | Long-form content: episodes, webinars, workshops | Audiences seeking depth | Authority + feeding the other platforms |
| WhatsApp Business | Intake and conversion channel | Prospective clients | Fast response and qualification |

## 5. Channel-by-Channel Strategy

### 5.1 X (formerly Twitter)

**Direction:** the legislative voice of Dr. Rashid Al Kaitoob. The primary platform for building authority and owning the trend.

**Accounts:**
- Dr. Rashid Al Kaitoob's personal account (primary).
- The firm's account (supporting; reposts and adds the institutional angle).

**What we publish:**

| Type | Frequency | Description |
|---|---|---|
| Immediate commentary on new legislation | Whenever a law, decree or decision is issued (within 2-6 hours) | A first tweet with the news + a thread of 5-10 tweets explaining what changed, who is affected, what to do |
| Weekly thread "A law in 10 tweets" | Once a week | Simplifying an existing law or an important article |
| Short legal opinion | 3-4 times a week | A single tweet with a clear position on a public issue with a legal dimension |
| Short vertical video (under 90 seconds) | 2 per week | Dr. Rashid explains one point on camera |
| Audio space (X Spaces) | Once a month | Open discussion on new legislation, with a guest |
| Repost with commentary | Daily | Engaging with official entities' news (Ministry of Justice, courts, economic authorities) |

**Tone rules on X:** composed, direct, no advertising language, promotes a service directly in no more than 1 of every 10 posts. Cites the law and article numbers precisely. Uses clear standard Arabic, with English where economic legislation calls for it.

**KPIs:** follower growth, impressions of legislative threads, number of quotes by journalists and verified accounts, number of times his name appears in the local trend or in the legislation conversation within 24 hours.

**The "Legislative Radar" mechanism:**

1. Daily (morning) scan of the Official Gazette, the Ministry of Justice and Supreme Legislation Committee websites, government entities' accounts and the UAE legislative portals.
2. When legislation is issued: the legal researcher prepares a one-page summary within one hour.
3. The content writer turns it into a thread and a video draft within one hour.
4. Dr. Rashid approves or amends (a dedicated WhatsApp channel for fast approval).
5. Publish on X first, then LinkedIn (institutional version), then Instagram (design + reel), then an article on the website within 24-48 hours to capture Google search.

### 5.2 Facebook

**Direction:** mass-market and broad, simplified language, direct search for individual clients and small-business owners.

**What we publish:**

| Type | Frequency |
|---|---|

- Retargeting of website visitors and viewers who watched more than 50% of a video.

**KPIs:** new weekly conversations, cost per lead, share of qualified leads from Facebook, video reach.

## 6. SEO and Google Reputation Plan

### 6.1 Foundation phase (months 1-2)

1. **Full technical audit:** indexing, site speed, mobile compatibility, errors, sitemap, robots file, structured data (Schema: LegalService, Attorney, Organization, FAQ, Article).
2. **Audit of existing content:** weak pages are deleted, merged or improved.
3. **Tool connections:** Google Search Console, Google Analytics 4, Google Business Profile, Bing Webmaster, a rank-tracking tool.
4. **Competitor analysis:** the 10 most visible law firms in the UAE in Arabic and English: their keywords, pages and backlinks.
5. **Keyword research and cluster building:** for each service, a cluster of one primary keyword + 10-30 secondary keywords + FAQs, in Arabic and English (sample in Appendix B).

### 6.2 Content build (ongoing from month 2)

| Type | Frequency | Goal |
|---|---|---|
| Optimised service page | 2 per month until all services are covered | Commercial-intent keywords ("real estate lawyer Dubai") |
| Long-form knowledge article (1,500-2,500 words) | 4 per month (1 per week) | Informational keywords ("how to file a labour claim") |
| New-legislation article (news/explainer) | On the event, within 48 hours | Capturing the search spike when a law is issued |
| FAQ page per service | 1 per month | Google Featured Snippets |
| Emirate/location pages (local pages) | Q2 | "lawyer in Abu Dhabi / Dubai / Sharjah ..." |
| Refreshing old articles | 4 per month | Holding positions |

**Quality standard:** every article is written or reviewed by a lawyer and signed in their name (E-E-A-T: experience, expertise, authoritativeness, trust), with an author page for each consultant linking to their biography and credentials.

### 6.3 Authority and backlinks (off-page)

- **Legal and business directories:** Legal 500, Chambers, UAE lawyer directories, trusted business directories; consistent data everywhere (name, address, phone).
- **Press publishing:** a press release or op-ed every month in an Arabic or English news outlet in the UAE, with a link to the website.
- **Guest posts:** 2 per month on business and legal platforms.
- **Media citations:** every media appearance (Section 13) includes a request for a link to the website or the founder's page.
- **Partnerships:** links from partners' websites, international law firms and associations.

### 6.4 Image on Google (reputation)

- **Google Business Profile:** 100% of fields completed, 20+ professional photos, a weekly post, an answer to every question, products/services listed.
- **Review request system:** after every successful case or consultation, the Account Manager sends a review link within 48 hours; target 12+ verified reviews per month; reply to every review within 24 hours in professional language without disclosing any details.
- **Branded search:** the first page for the firm's name and Dr. Rashid Al Kaitoob's name must be 100% under control: website, LinkedIn, X, Instagram, YouTube, interviews, press articles, Business Profile. Monthly monitoring, pushing any unwanted result to the second page through content.
- **Founder's Knowledge Panel:** an official biography page on the website, a Wikidata entry, consistent name and photo, press articles naming him with his title, Person Schema markup.
- **Alerts:** Google Alerts and daily monitoring of name mentions across platforms.

## 7. Content Types and the Production System

### 7.1 Content types and target weekly volume (by Q2)

| Type | Weekly volume | Platforms | Production notes |
|---|---|---|---|
| Static designs | 6-8 | Instagram, LinkedIn, Facebook, X | Approved brand templates |
| Carousels | 2-3 | Instagram, LinkedIn | 5-8 slides each |
| Reels / short vertical video | 4-5 | Instagram, Facebook, YouTube Shorts, X, TikTok (optional, by management decision) | Batch recording on one day per week |
| Long-form video | 0.5 (2 per month) | YouTube, website | Webinar, episode or interview |
| Motion graphics / animated visuals | 1-2 | All platforms | For every new law + occasions |
| Stories | 15-25 | Instagram, Facebook | Daily |
| Tweets and threads | 15-20 tweets + 1-2 threads | X | From the founder's and the firm's accounts |
| Articles | 1 long-form + 1-2 short | Website, LinkedIn | Mandatory legal review |
| Email / LinkedIn newsletter | 1 per month | Email, LinkedIn | Legislative updates |
| Webinar | 1 per month | Zoom/LinkedIn Live + YouTube | Pre-registration = leads |
| In-person or hybrid workshop | 1 per quarter | The office / a partner venue | For corporates and investors |
| Podcast episode | 2 per month | Audio platforms, YouTube | Cut into 6-10 clips |
| Live stream | 1-2 per month | Instagram, Facebook, X Spaces | Audience questions |
| Occasion content | Per the calendar | All | Prepared 2 weeks ahead |

### 7.2 Production pipeline

```
Idea/legislation → legal summary (researcher) → script/copy (content writer)
→ legal review (consultant) → production (designer/editor) → approval (Marketing Manager; the founder for his own content)
→ scheduling (publishing tool) → publish → engagement and replies (coordinator) → weekly measurement → recycle the best
```

**The "1 to 7" rule:** every core idea produces at least 7 assets: a thread, a carousel, a reel, a static design, an article, a story, a LinkedIn post.

**Batch recording:** a fixed day each week (for example Tuesday) to record all of the coming week's videos with the consultants; and a long monthly session with Dr. Rashid (two hours) to record 4 weeks of content.

### 7.3 Identity and tone

- **Visual identity guide:** colours, Arabic and English fonts, templates per platform, photography style, a light watermark.
- **Tone guide:** for the firm: professional, confident, warm, precise. For the founder: leading, analytical, direct, uncompromising on what is right.
- **Prohibited list:** no promises of outcomes, no details of ongoing cases, no client names without written consent, no disparaging comparisons with competitors, no political opinions outside the legal frame.

## 12. Lead Generation Funnel and Sales Handoff

### 12.1 Stages

```
Awareness      → Reels, threads, videos, SEO, media appearances
Interest       → articles, guides, webinars, newsletters
Consideration  → case studies, reviews, team page, service page
Action         → form / WhatsApp / call / consultation booking
Qualification  → 3 qualifying questions + A/B/C classification
Handoff        → CRM → BD Assistant / Sales within one hour
Close & retain → Sales + Account Manager; review request; newsletter enrolment
```

### 12.2 Capture points

- Website forms and landing pages.
- WhatsApp Business (linked from every platform).
- Lead Ads on Facebook and Instagram.
- Webinar and workshop registrations.
- Guide (PDF) downloads in exchange for an email address.
- Direct messages on the platforms.
- Phone calls (a dedicated tracking number for digital).

### 12.3 Qualification and classification

| Class | Criterion | Action |
|---|---|---|
| A (hot) | A company or individual with a specific matter, a budget and a timeline | Call from Sales within 60 minutes |
| B (warm) | A clear need without urgency | Call within 24 hours + send a suitable guide |
| C (cold) | A general or educational enquiry | Informational reply + add to the newsletter and retargeting |

### 12.4 Marketing-to-Sales service agreement

- Marketing hands over every lead with its source, service type and qualification notes in the CRM within one hour of receipt.
- Sales updates the status of every lead within 48 hours (contacted / meeting / proposal / closed / lost and why).
- A weekly 15-minute quality meeting: which leads were truly qualified, and which channel produces the best quality.
- Sales gives Marketing the 10 most frequent client questions each month; these become content.

### 12.5 Paid advertising (operating principle)

- Start in Q2 after organic content has stabilised.
- Only 3 services at first (highest margin and highest demand).
- Measure by cost per qualified lead, not by clicks.
- Rule: any campaign that fails to achieve an acceptable cost after 14 days is adjusted or stopped.

## 17. Governance, Compliance and Risk

### 17.1 Legal and professional compliance

- Compliance with the rules and ethics of the legal profession in the UAE regarding advertising and promotion of legal services (Ministry of Justice and the competent local bodies such as Dubai's Legal Affairs Department); advertising copy is reviewed by a legal consultant before publication.
- Compliance with UAE media and digital-content legislation, including licensing requirements for paid advertising content and dealings with influencers.
- Personal data protection under the federal data protection law; explicit consent for newsletters and broadcast lists.
- Absolute client confidentiality: no publication of any case or client information without written consent; case studies are fully anonymised.
- A standard disclaimer on educational content: "This content is for general awareness and does not constitute legal advice."

### 17.2 Approval authorities

| Content type | Approved by |
|---|---|
| General educational content | Marketing Manager after legal review |
| Commentary on new legislation | The specialised consultant + Marketing Manager; for the founder: Dr. Rashid personally |
| Content mentioning clients or partners | Head of BD + the party's written consent |
| Press releases | General Manager |
| Responses to crises or public criticism | General Manager + Marketing Manager within two hours |

### 17.3 Risk and crisis management

| Risk | Prevention | Response |
|---|---|---|
| Legal error in a post | Mandatory legal review | Delete/correct within one hour + public clarification if needed |
| Negative comments or a bad review | Daily monitoring | Professional reply within 24 hours, move the discussion to private, fix the root cause |
| Information leak | Limited permissions, two-factor authentication (in coordination with IT) | IT incident protocol + statement |
| Account breach | Two-factor authentication, managed passwords, role-based permissions | Immediate recovery + notify followers |
| Founder's commentary misunderstood | Review wording with the consultant before publishing; avoid ongoing cases | Quick clarification from the same account |
| Declining ad performance | The 14-day rule | Adjust or stop |
| Over-reliance on one person | Documented processes, templates, deputies | - |

### 17.4 Critical dependencies for the plan's success

1. Hiring the Sales & Marketing Manager in month 1.
2. Dr. Rashid Al Kaitoob's commitment to a monthly recording session (two hours) and a fast approval channel (reply within two hours on legislative commentary).
3. The Legal Department's commitment to reviewing content within 24 hours (and within one hour for new legislation).
4. Connecting the website and WhatsApp to the CRM in coordination with IT in Q1.
5. Quarterly budget approval.
